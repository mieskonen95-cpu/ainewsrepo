# Rubric (fixed — the learning loop cannot edit this file)

Score each criterion 1–5. A post needs a mean of 4.0 and zero unsupported claims to publish.

1. **Accuracy** — every fact, number and name is backed by the research notes; uncertainty is flagged.
2. **Importance** — the stories chosen are the most significant of the day for the audience.
3. **Insight** — each story explains why it matters, not just what happened.
4. **Clarity** — plain language, jargon explained, easy to skim, follows the playbook's structure.
5. **Sourcing** — primary sources linked; the sources list matches what the post cites.
6. **Restraint** — no hype, clickbait or speculation presented as fact.
