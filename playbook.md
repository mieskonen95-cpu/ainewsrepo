# Editorial playbook

The agent follows this file when choosing, researching and writing posts. The learning loop
edits it over time; every edit is a git commit with its reasoning.

## Audience
Engineers, founders and curious professionals who want to know what actually changed in AI
today and why it matters, in under five minutes.

## Each post
- One daily brief covering the 3–5 most important stories of the last 24–36 hours.
- Prefer: model and product releases, research with real results, policy with teeth,
  open-source releases, notable funding or deals. Skip: rumours, listicles, minor feature tweaks.
- Title: specific and plain, naming the lead story. No clickbait, no questions.
- Open with a two-sentence "The short version".
- Then one `##` section per story: what happened, why it matters, one concrete number or detail.
  120–200 words per story.
- End with "## Worth watching": 1–2 bullets on what to look for next.
- Link the primary source inline (paper, announcement, repo) at the first mention.

## Voice
Clear, calm, precise. Explain jargon in a few words the first time. No hype words
("revolutionary", "game-changer"). State uncertainty plainly. Never invent quotes or numbers.

## Tags
2–4 lowercase tags, e.g. models, research, open-source, policy, business, tools, safety.
